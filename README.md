# Human–AI Contribution Provenance

**HACP** is an experimental open specification for describing how humans and generative AI contributed to digital content.

Instead of reducing content provenance to a binary question such as:

> "Was this generated by AI?"

HACP describes the roles played by humans and AI systems.

For example:

- a human provided the original idea and expertise,
- an AI system helped structure the content,
- an AI system improved wording,
- a human reviewed and approved the final result.

The goal is not to detect AI-generated content.

The goal is to make AI involvement transparent, structured and verifiable.

---

## Why?

The distinction between "human content" and "AI-generated content" is increasingly insufficient.

Consider these examples:

### Human authored

A person writes an article without generative AI assistance.

### AI assisted

A person provides the idea, expertise and facts.

An AI system helps with:

- grammar,
- wording,
- structure,
- translation.

### Human–AI collaboration

A human and an AI system jointly develop:

- arguments,
- structure,
- examples,
- wording.

### AI generated

A system generates most or all of the content from a prompt with little human involvement.

These scenarios should not be represented by the same label.
